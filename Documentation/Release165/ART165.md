> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production background illustration for the navigation hub of Second Dimension: Guild of Worlds, a polished anime-inspired fantasy mobile RPG. Create one wide 16:9 richly painted landscape of a luminous ancient guild waystation overlooking a magical world at dusk. A warm lantern-lit guild town and market in the lower right, a beautiful winding adventure road and archway across the left, an impossibly tall blue-violet tower rising in the far left distance, and monumental ancient titan statues half veiled in clouds at the upper right. Foreground: an elegant circular stone portal dais and carved brass navigation compass, framing the scene without filling the center. Sophisticated teal, deep indigo, warm amber and soft gold palette, cinematic depth, detailed hand-painted environment, strong readable silhouettes at mobile size, inviting adventure. This is the actual image beneath four large interactive destination cards, so keep the central middle 70 percent quieter with atmospheric negative space and concentrate interest toward edges and top. No characters, no lettering, no words, no logos, no icons, no menu buttons, no UI mockup, no branching node graph, no spiderweb routes. High-quality finished game environment art.

Rendered navigation acceptance is recorded separately after integration. PNG is loaded from StreamingAssets and included in both delivery copies and the source handoff.
