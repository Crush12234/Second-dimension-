SECOND DIMENSION - ALPHA TEST 2.0
Update 165: Campaign cards, Unions, illustrated Loops, and specialist merchants

The upgraded card adventure begins in Quest 1. Quests 1, 2 and 3 now offer
explicit D20 blessings/hexes and Fortune Wheel events. Choose a card, roll or
spin, then collect. Saved results survive changing loops and restarting.
Authored story skill checks still use their original two six-sided dice.
Completed quests and collected rewards are preserved; no progress is reset.

Quest 3 uses illustrated locations instead of the retired spiderweb node map.
The new Waystation screen has illustrated destinations, saved-progress labels,
and direct shortcuts including BUILD UNIONS.

ADD UNION is visible in the Union planner. Owned reserve heroes can fill new
teams. New teams join upcoming opening-quest battles. A later adventure whose
deployment is already sealed keeps that deployment until the next adventure.
An already committed battle is preserved exactly.

MERCHANT SPECIALTIES
Mera: provisions, luck tonics, armor and shields.
Dorrik: weapons and armor commissions.
Tavi: accessories that fit either accessory slot.
Sella: authored heroes and duplicate growth for ascension or arts.
Orven: magical weapons and relics.
Higher Merchant Row levels retain improved equipment quality and expanded
stock; hero offers progress from B to A to S rank.

TRAVEL POUCH
Purchase a Wayglass Luck Tonic from Mera, then activate it in TRAVEL POUCH.
One active tonic improves the next unsealed blessing: roll two D20s and keep
the higher. The charge is consumed when that blessing is chosen. Existing
sealed or rolled results remain unchanged. The natural-20 chance is 9.75%
instead of 5%. Wheel and curse outcomes are unchanged.

SAVES AND SOURCE
This update preserves each copy's own Game/SaveData folder. The personal
playable keeps its existing folder and shortcut for continuity; its internal
version is now update165. The alpha package includes versioned C# source
restoration instructions and a new source patch. See Developer Source.

Please report the quest, loop, action, expected result and actual result when
submitting feedback. Never delete SaveData to install a later update.
