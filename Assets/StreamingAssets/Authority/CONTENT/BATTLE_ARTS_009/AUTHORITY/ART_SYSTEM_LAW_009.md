# Art System Law 009

1. **Weapon identity:** a normal recruit’s fixed weapon family points to one Combat Art tree. Equipping a matching weapon makes learned nodes legal; unequipping it preserves mastery but removes those Arts from legal Forecast pools.
2. **Learn by meaningful use:** actual successful weapon attacks, casts, heals, barriers, rescues, interruptions, and protection advance the relevant Art. No progress is granted for trivial enemies or no-effect spam.
3. **Union command model:** the player selects a complete contextual Forecast. Exact member Arts are visible predictions and cannot be clicked.
4. **Mixed roles:** one Forecast may contain a spear Art, axe Art, Flame Mystic, Restoration Art, and Guard action when the Union composition, AP, MP, targets, and battle state make that plan legal.
5. **Costs:** AP is shared by the Union. MP is personal. One member can never pay another member’s MP.
6. **Breakthroughs:** an eligible learned-by-use breakthrough may substitute a stronger legal Art during resolution, using deterministic seeded authority and a guaranteed threshold.
7. **Presentation:** weapon families and Mystic schools share reusable motion grammar, but each Art has authored timing, camera, VFX, SFX, impact, target, and consequence profiles.
8. **Kael:** Kael never enters ordinary weapon, Mystic, equipment, class, or mastery progression. No Ward, counter, mirror, or relic effect may imitate True Reflection.
